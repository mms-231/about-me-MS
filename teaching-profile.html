Introduction

Hello! I’m Mary Schlueter. I am a student at Brigham Young University studying teaching and education. This page highlights my teaching philosophy, writing practices, lesson planning, and professional background.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe that the most meaningful learning is learning that can be applied to the real world. When students are able to look at their surroundings and make sense of them through what they have learned, education becomes valuable and lasting.

I want to create a classroom environment that is calm, intentional, and student-centered. Rather than filling the room with distractions, I prefer to let student work shape the space over time. At the same time, I think it is important that students see me as a real person, so I would include small elements that reflect who I am.

I value a classroom where students are given the freedom to work at their own pace while still receiving consistent guidance and support. I want to check in frequently, create open communication, and build a space where students feel comfortable asking questions.

Above all, I want to help students care about learning. By setting high expectations and supporting their growth, I aim to help them recognize their own potential.

Writing Philosophy

Good writing is writing that accomplishes something for the reader. Whether it makes someone smile, think differently, or feel something new, effective writing fulfills its purpose. I value writing that is intentional, engaging, and meaningful to its audience.

Creative Process

When I begin writing, I like to talk through my ideas with others and use conversation as a way to generate and refine thoughts. If something stands out or feels interesting, I write it down and begin narrowing or combining ideas.

When working independently, I guide my thinking with questions such as: Who is my audience? What is my purpose? How can I present this in a way that is engaging and clear? I also consider what will make the writing process feel manageable and meaningful.

Theory of Learning

A strong learning environment should minimize distractions and promote focus. When distractions are present, they should be relevant to the learning taking place.

I have found that consistency and familiarity play an important role in successful learning. Having a reliable space to work and return to can reduce stress and improve focus.

As a teacher, I want to create an environment that offers both stability and comfort. By maintaining consistent elements in the classroom, students can feel secure and supported.

Writing Program

My writing program focuses on helping students develop confidence, clarity, and purpose in their writing. Students engage in personal, analytical, and creative writing while learning to adapt their voice for different audiences.

  • Writing as a process (brainstorming, drafting, revising, editing)
  • Audience awareness and purpose
  • Peer collaboration and feedback
  • Real-world writing applications

Sample Lesson Plan

Lesson Title: Writing for Audience and Purpose

Grade Level: High School (9–10)

Time: 50 minutes

Objective:
Students will identify how audience and purpose shape writing and apply these concepts in a short writing activity.

Activities:

  • Warm-Up: Compare texting a friend vs emailing a teacher
  • Mini Lesson: Discuss audience and purpose
  • Group Activity: Rewrite for a different audience
  • Independent Writing: Create a short paragraph
  • Wrap-Up: Share and reflect

Assessment:
Participation and written responses.
